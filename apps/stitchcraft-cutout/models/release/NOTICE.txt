StitchCraft's subject cut-out models, release cutout-models-1
=============================================================

These are the 3 models that StitchCraft's `stitch plan --subject` and `stitch cutout` run to cut a photo's
subject out before VectorCraft traces it (https://github.com/KaboomFox/stitchcraft, ADR-0019). StitchCraft's
own code is under the MIT License or the Apache License, Version 2.0, at your option. These files are their
authors' models, under the licences below, and StitchCraft didn't train or change them.

u2netp.onnx
-----------
U²-Net-p, the small model of U²-Net, by Xuebin Qin, Zichen Zhang, Chenyang Huang, Masood Dehghan, Osmar
Zaiane and Martin Jagersand ("U2-Net: Going Deeper with Nested U-Structure for Salient Object Detection",
Pattern Recognition 106, 107404, 2020), https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net.

Licence: the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE-U-2-Net.txt). U²-Net's repository is under it, and its
author wrote in its issue 208, in May 2021, that "all the released codes and models in this repo follow the
same license currently". The model was trained on the DUTS dataset.

This file is rembg's conversion of the author's checkpoint, u2netp.pth, to ONNX, as rembg publishes it at
https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg/releases/download/v0.0.0/u2netp.onnx. rembg is Copyright (c) 2020
Daniel Gatis, under the MIT License (LICENSE-rembg.txt). The author publishes the checkpoint itself on
Google Drive and Baidu only. The file is released here as rembg publishes it.

mobilesam-encoder.onnx and mobilesam-decoder.onnx
-------------------------------------------------
MobileSAM, by Chaoning Zhang, Dongshen Han, Yu Qiao, Jung Uk Kim, Sung-Ho Bae, Seungkyu Lee and Choong Seon
Hong ("Faster Segment Anything: Towards Lightweight SAM for Mobile Applications", arXiv:2306.14289, 2023),
https://github.com/ChaoningZhang/MobileSAM. MobileSAM was distilled from Segment Anything, by Alexander
Kirillov and others at Meta AI, and keeps its prompt encoder and mask decoder.

Licence: the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE-MobileSAM.txt), which MobileSAM's repository is under,
its weights, weights/mobile_sam.pt, among it.

StitchCraft exported both files from that checkpoint, at the repository's commit f706ad9, with MobileSAM's
own code and PyTorch's ONNX exporter (export.py, EXPORT.md): the image encoder, and the prompt encoder and
mask decoder as MobileSAM's SamOnnxModel puts them together. Their weights are the checkpoint's.
