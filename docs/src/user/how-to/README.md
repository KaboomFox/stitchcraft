# How-to guides

Each guide solves one problem:

- [Check a machine file before sewing](check-a-file.md) with `stitch inspect` and `stitch preview`
- [Convert between machine formats](convert-formats.md) with `stitch convert`
- [Fit a design to the hoop](fit-the-hoop.md) when it is larger than the hoop or the comfort zone
- [Embroider a VectorCraft design](embroider-a-vectorcraft-design.md) with the plug-ins and
  `stitch export`
- [Make satin columns and keep presets in VectorCraft](vectorcraft-columns-and-presets.md) with
  VectorCraft's own commands and Graphic Styles
- [Route satin columns](route-satin-columns.md) in VectorCraft, as Ink/Stitch's Auto-Route Satin Columns
  routes them
- [Route running stitches](route-running-stitches.md) in VectorCraft, as Ink/Stitch's Auto-Route Running
  Stitch routes them
- [Sew underlays first](sew-underlays-first.md) where satin columns cross, or a border lies over a fill
- [Set text in an embroidery font](set-text-in-a-font.md) with `stitch lettering`
- [Cut a photo's subject out](cut-out-a-photos-subject.md) before it is traced, so that its background
  isn't sewn (`stitch plan --subject`)
- [Keep small parts of a picture](keep-small-parts.md), such as an eye, from merging into their
  neighbours, with a mark
- [Make the rows of a fill follow a form](guide-the-rows.md), such as fur or a petal, with a line drawn
  over the picture
- [Find a shape by its name](find-a-shape.md) in VectorCraft's Layers panel, the notices and the report
- [Expand an automatic design](expand-an-automatic-design.md) into shapes you edit one by one
  (`stitch expand`)
- [Report a bug](report-a-bug.md) with a bundle that reproduces it (`stitch bug-report`)
