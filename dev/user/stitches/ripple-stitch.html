Ripple stitch

A ripple stitch sews lines that spread out from a stroke. Copies of its line shrink towards a point or follow a guide, or lines run along a satin column from one of its edges to the other. Every line is a running stitch, and the whole ripple sews as one run. Use it for light textures that fade to a point, such as a shell or the rings round a flower’s heart. With a guide, it repeats a pattern along a curve. Set an element’s stroke_method to ripple_stitch for it.

In VectorCraft, set the StitchCraft Stroke effect’s stroke_method to ripple_stitch. In an SVG file, the line’s settings are Ink/Stitch’s attributes (setting parameters).

Kinds of ripple

The stroke’s lines give the kind of ripple:

  • An open line ripples towards its target point. Each copy of the line is the one before it shrunk towards the point, and the last copy shrinks to the point. With a guide, its copies follow the guide instead.
  • A closed line spirals in to its target point, each turn a smaller copy of the line. With a guide, its copies follow the guide.
  • 2 lines or more are a satin column’s rails and rungs, drawn as for a satin column. The first line lies on the first rail and the last on the second, and the lines between follow both.

A part of the path that is one point sews nothing, and SC-W0401 names it.

The target point

In an SVG file, Ink/Stitch’s target point command tied to the stroke places the point the ripple shrinks to. In a VectorCraft document, a small path named stitch:target grouped with the stroke places it, at the middle of the box round its anchors. Without either, the target point is the middle of the area the line spans, the centroid of its convex hull. A ripple along a column has no target point.

How many lines

line_count is the number of lines, 10 by default, the stroke’s own line and the target point among them. With min_line_dist_mm, the count is the shortest distance from the line to the target point, or across the column, divided by it. The count keeps the parity of line_count, odd or even, so that the ripple ends on the same side however large it is drawn. A ripple has 1,000 lines at most (SC-W0407).

exponent spaces the lines. At 1 they lie evenly apart, and above 1 they crowd towards the first line. flip_exponent crowds them towards the last line instead.

Evenly spacedExponent 2Exponent 2, flipped

skip_start leaves out the first lines, such as the stroke’s own line, and skip_end the last, such as the lines that crowd round the target point. Skips that together would leave out every line leave out none (SC-W0408).

Joins

With flip_copies on, the default, every other line is sewn backwards, and the ripple goes back and forth. join_style sets how each line meets the next. With the flat join, 0 and the default, a straight run of stitches joins each line’s end to the next line’s start. With the point join, 1, each line bends into the next so that they meet at a point, and the ripple has a line more to bend.

With flip_copies off, every line is sewn the same way, and the needle goes straight from each line’s end to the next line’s start. A spiral is always sewn so.

Staggers

staggers moves where each line’s needle points fall, so that the needle points of lines side by side don’t line up. Each line’s needle points start a staggers-th of the stitch length further back than the line’s before. At 0, every line is placed from the end it is sewn from. The default, 1, places every line’s needle points from its start. Ink/Stitch’s settings window shows 0, and it sews a file that sets none as 1.

Guides

A guide makes a ripple’s copies of its line follow it, in place of shrinking towards a point. In an SVG file, Ink/Stitch’s Selection to guide line makes a path a guide line: it starts the path with Ink/Stitch’s guide-line marker. The guide steers the strokes grouped with it, and is not sewn. In a VectorCraft document, name a path grouped with the stroke stitch:guide. TS-17C sews a guided ripple of each kind:

Along a guide line

A guide of one line moves the copies along it, from its start to its end. Each copy moves to its place along the guide, and with rotate_ripples on, the default, it turns as the guide turns. The copies grow or shrink evenly from scale_start to scale_end percent, 100 and 0 by default, so that they shrink to nothing at the guide’s end. scale_axis scales them across alone, 1, down alone, 2, or not at all, 3. line_count, min_line_dist_mm and exponent place the copies along the guide as they place a ripple’s lines. With satin_guide_pattern_position set to render_at_rungs, a copy lies at each point of the guide instead. Ink/Stitch asks for such a guide to be a manual stitch, whose points are its nodes. A guide of curves has a point wherever it is flattened.

Turning, to 40 %Not turningTurning, full size

Between a satin guide’s rails

A guide drawn as a satin column, with 2 rails and rungs, sets each copy across it: the copy turns and stretches so that the ends of the line lie on the 2 rails. The ripple’s reverse_rails and swap_satin_rails say how its rails are read. satin_guide_pattern_position places the copies:

  • Line count, the default, spreads them evenly along the band, as many as line_count or min_line_dist_mm gives.
  • At the rungs sets one at the end of each section between rungs.
  • Adaptive sets each one as soon as the box round it clears the one before by min_line_dist_mm. That holds where the guide runs with its first rail on the left, as Ink/Stitch places them. With the first rail on the right, the boxes overlap by min_line_dist_mm instead.

A closed line, such as a small circle, is centred between the rails instead. It grows and shrinks as the band widens and narrows, and turns as the band turns when rotate_ripples is on.

The anchor line

An anchor line sets which points of the line lie on a satin guide’s rails: the start and the end of the anchor line, in place of the line’s own ends. Draw it across the line, and Ink/Stitch’s Selection to anchor line marks it in an SVG file. In a VectorCraft document, name a path grouped with the stroke stitch:anchor. A closed line with an anchor line is stretched across the rails so too, not centred.

Unusable guides

A guide line of no length can’t place copies, and neither can a satin guide whose rails can’t be read. SC-W0409 says why, and the ripple sews as if it had no guide, towards its target point. An anchor line whose ends are one point is left out the same way.

Grids

grid_size_mm adds a grid: lines across the ripple’s own, each through the same point of every line. The needle points of the line, or the pairs across a column, then lie the grid size apart, and so do the grid’s lines. The grid sews after the ripple as a run of its own, starting at its corner nearest where the ripple ended. grid_first sews the grid first, and the whole ripple backwards.

Placing the copies by hand

With manual_pattern_placement on, the copies are made of the nodes of the line as you drew them, in place of its running stitch. Each copy is sewn through its own nodes, and a straight run of even stitches joins it to the next. With flip_copies on, every other copy is sewn backwards, the first among them.

Clip paths

A ripple cut to clip paths sews only what lies inside them. Its lines cross the clip’s edges again and again, so StitchCraft sews the pieces inside as a fill sews its rows, going along the clip’s edge from one to the next, without a jump between them. A ripple wholly outside its clip paths isn’t sewn, and SC-W0013 says so.

Stitches, repeats and bean stitch

Each line is sewn with the running stitch’s running_stitch_length_mm and running_stitch_tolerance_mm, and with random stitch lengths when they are on. reverse sews the whole ripple backwards. repeats sews each run there and back, and bean_stitch_repeats then sews it as it sews a running stitch. Lines near the target point can be shorter than the shortest stitch the machine sews well, and StitchCraft leaves their needle points out when it finishes the plan (SC-I0504).

Not applied

A ripple along a column reads its rails without push_compensation_mm, which Ink/Stitch applies to them, and SC-W0406 names it when it is set.
